# EN03 OA 10 — English sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues
| Nivel | Asignatura | Tema/eje | Cobertura | Dosificación |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3° básico | Inglés (Propuesta) | Expresión oral | propuesta-mineduc | 5 clases de 90 min |

## Qué se debe aprender
> Reproducir chants, rimas y diálogos muy breves y simples para familiarizarse con los sonidos propios del inglés: identificar los sonidos /w/, /th/ en particular y /s/-/z/ en particular.

> **Derechos y procedencia:** el código y la redacción del OA anterior proceden de
> Currículum Nacional (MINEDUC) y se reproducen con enlace y fecha de consulta para
> trazabilidad. No se declaran obra del proyecto ni quedan cubiertos por la licencia
> CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 del contenido pedagógico original. Consulta
> [LICENSING.md](../../../LICENSING.md).

**Explicación pedagógica.** English sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues se desarrolla mediante experiencias propias de la disciplina. La secuencia parte de repeat short chunks with rhythm and intelligibility y enfrenta la confusión «equate pronunciation with intelligence or demand imitation of one accent».

**Antes de comenzar.** repeat short chunks with rhythm and intelligibility

**Vocabulario explícito:** sound, mouth position, voice, vibration, /w/, /th/, /s/, /z/.

### Clase 1 de 5: Notice and produce /w/ {#cl-02533}
**Estado editorial:** Desarrollada con contenido específico.

**Propósito docente:** Enseñar english sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues a través de «notice and produce /w/», con una experiencia disciplinar específica, segura y revisable.

**Meta para estudiantes:** Hoy desarrollaré «notice and produce /w/» y mostraré una decisión propia con evidencia.

| Momento | Tiempo | Acción docente y experiencia |
|---|---:|---|
| Inicio | 10 min | Presenta una vez teacher model and brief authorized recordings, never accent rankings para «notice and produce /w/», con gesto u objeto y sin traducir completo. Cada estudiante responde mediante acción, elección, marca o chunk breve. |
| Modelado | 20 min | Think aloud with accessible English and Spanish only when needed: model notice and produce /w/ and point to the sound, word, image or pattern that carries meaning. Contrast the misconception: equate pronunciation with intelligence or demand imitation of one accent. |
| Práctica guiada | 25 min | Practice «notice and produce /w/» through echo, information gap, sorting or short turns; change one meaningful detail so students listen, read or compose instead of reciting. |
| Desempeño individual | 25 min | Complete a new and culturally safe task for «notice and produce /w/»; select language support, communicate meaning and revise after checking the input. |
| Cierre | 10 min | For «notice and produce /w/», give an action, choice, spoken chunk or short written response and point to the clue that supports it. |

**Materiales y preparación:** teacher model and brief authorized recordings, never accent rankings; visible word bank, picture cards and optional audio replay; no account or personal information required.

### Insumo concreto y consigna

**Recurso listo para usar:** Original classroom dialogue: “A: What do we need for the school garden? B: We need a clear plan. A: I can bring the list. B: Great. Let's check it together.”

**Consigna exacta:** Use the dialogue to work on “notice and produce /w/”. Underline two useful chunks, change one detail and perform or write a new exchange that keeps the meaning clear.

**Referencia para modelar y corregir:** A successful response reuses two meaningful chunks, changes the place or contribution coherently and remains understandable; accent imitation is not required.

### Clase 5 de 5: Perform a brief intelligible dialogue {#cl-02537}
**Estado editorial:** Desarrollada con contenido específico.

**Propósito docente:** Enseñar english sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues a través de «perform a brief intelligible dialogue», con una experiencia disciplinar específica, segura y revisable.

**Meta para estudiantes:** Hoy desarrollaré «perform a brief intelligible dialogue» y mostraré una decisión propia con evidencia.

| Momento | Tiempo | Acción docente y experiencia |
|---|---:|---|
| Inicio | 10 min | Presenta una vez teacher model and brief authorized recordings, never accent rankings para «perform a brief intelligible dialogue», con gesto u objeto y sin traducir completo. Cada estudiante responde mediante acción, elección, marca o chunk breve. |
| Modelado | 20 min | Think aloud with accessible English and Spanish only when needed: model perform a brief intelligible dialogue and point to the sound, word, image or pattern that carries meaning. Contrast the misconception: equate pronunciation with intelligence or demand imitation of one accent. |
| Práctica guiada | 25 min | Practice «perform a brief intelligible dialogue» through echo, information gap, sorting or short turns; change one meaningful detail so students listen, read or compose instead of reciting. |
| Desempeño individual | 25 min | Complete a new and culturally safe task for «perform a brief intelligible dialogue»; select language support, communicate meaning and revise after checking the input. |
| Cierre | 10 min | For «perform a brief intelligible dialogue», give an action, choice, spoken chunk or short written response and point to the clue that supports it. |

**Materiales y preparación:** teacher model and brief authorized recordings, never accent rankings; visible word bank, picture cards and optional audio replay; no account or personal information required.

### Insumo concreto y consigna

**Recurso listo para usar:** Original classroom dialogue: “A: What do we need for the library? B: We need a clear plan. A: I can bring the list. B: Great. Let's check it together.”

**Consigna exacta:** Use the dialogue to work on “perform a brief intelligible dialogue”. Underline two useful chunks, change one detail and perform or write a new exchange that keeps the meaning clear.

**Referencia para modelar y corregir:** A successful response reuses two meaningful chunks, changes the place or contribution coherently and remains understandable; accent imitation is not required.

**Lista de preparación docente:**

**Profundización:** Change speaker, audience, quantity, place or action and adapt the message to the new communicative purpose.

**Evidencia:** Comprehensible oral, written, visual or embodied response for «perform a brief intelligible dialogue» linked to a word, chunk or clue in the input.

**Criterios de éxito:** resuelve la consigna exacta de «perform a brief intelligible dialogue»; usa al menos dos datos, rasgos o evidencias del insumo concreto; explica una decisión y revisa su respuesta ante una condición nueva.
