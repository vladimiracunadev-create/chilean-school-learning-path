# EN01 OA 10 — English sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues
| Nivel | Asignatura | Tema/eje | Cobertura | Dosificación |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1° básico | Inglés (Propuesta) | Expresión oral | propuesta-mineduc | 5 clases de 90 min |

## Qué se debe aprender
> Reproducir chants, rimas y diálogos muy breves y simples para familiarizarse con los sonidos propios del inglés: /w/, /th/ en particular y /s/-/z/ en particular.

> **Derechos y procedencia:** el código y la redacción del OA anterior proceden de
> Currículum Nacional (MINEDUC) y se reproducen con enlace y fecha de consulta para
> trazabilidad. No se declaran obra del proyecto ni quedan cubiertos por la licencia
> CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 del contenido pedagógico original. Consulta
> [LICENSING.md](../../../LICENSING.md).

**Explicación pedagógica.** La secuencia desarrolla english sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues mediante experiencias disciplinares, práctica con retroalimentación y evidencia individual. Controla explícitamente el riesgo de que el estudiante repeats louder rather than adjusting mouth, airflow or voicing.

**Antes de comenzar.** Participar en una experiencia breve, atender una demostración y comunicar una primera respuesta por una vía accesible; no se exige dominio previo ni exposición de información privada.

**Vocabulario explícito:** english sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues, evidencia, decisión, práctica, seguridad, comunicar y revisar.

### Clase 1 de 5: Hear and see /w/ {#cl-00399}
**Estado editorial:** Desarrollada con contenido específico.

**Propósito docente:** Enseñar english sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues a través de «hear and see /w/», con una experiencia propia de Inglés (Propuesta) y evidencia verificable.

**Meta para estudiantes:** Hoy I will shape rounded lips and glide.

| Momento | Tiempo | Acción docente y experiencia |
|---|---:|---|
| Inicio | 10 min | Use a short image, object or line of English connected to «hear and see /w/». Students first respond by pointing, acting or choosing; the teacher notes who is relying on the shortcut «repeats louder rather than adjusting mouth, airflow or voicing» without public correction. |
| Modelado | 20 min | Model meaning before terminology: Contrast we with a Spanish-like initial, three with tree as an approximation goal, and sip with zip by noticing lip shape, airflow and voice. Think aloud in accessible Spanish when needed, then replay or reread the English clue and show exactly how it supports «I will shape rounded lips and glide». |
| Práctica guiada | 25 min | Pairs complete an information gap about «hear and see /w/»: one listens, reads or speaks and the other acts, selects or completes. They switch roles, ask for repetition and change one word so meaning—not memorization—must guide the response. |
| Desempeño individual | 25 min | Each student completes a fresh listening, speaking, reading or writing task for «I will shape rounded lips and glide». Gesture, picture or word bank may support access, but the final choice or message must be their own. |
| Cierre | 10 min | With one new prompt, demonstrate «I will shape rounded lips and glide» by acting, selecting, saying or writing. Point to the heard, read or visual clue and repair one part after feedback. |

**Materiales y preparación:** mirror, sound cards, short chant and respectful pronunciation recording

### Insumo concreto y consigna

### Clase 5 de 5: Use a tiny dialogue {#cl-00403}
**Estado editorial:** Desarrollada con contenido específico.

**Propósito docente:** Enseñar english sounds through chants, rhymes and dialogues a través de «use a tiny dialogue», con una experiencia propia de Inglés (Propuesta) y evidencia verificable.

**Meta para estudiantes:** Hoy I will communicate without demanding native accent.

| Momento | Tiempo | Acción docente y experiencia |
|---|---:|---|
| Inicio | 10 min | Use a short image, object or line of English connected to «use a tiny dialogue». Students first respond by pointing, acting or choosing; the teacher notes who is relying on the shortcut «repeats louder rather than adjusting mouth, airflow or voicing» without public correction. |
| Modelado | 20 min | Model meaning before terminology: Contrast we with a Spanish-like initial, three with tree as an approximation goal, and sip with zip by noticing lip shape, airflow and voice. Think aloud in accessible Spanish when needed, then replay or reread the English clue and show exactly how it supports «I will communicate without demanding native accent». |
| Práctica guiada | 25 min | Pairs complete an information gap about «use a tiny dialogue»: one listens, reads or speaks and the other acts, selects or completes. They switch roles, ask for repetition and change one word so meaning—not memorization—must guide the response. |
| Desempeño individual | 25 min | Each student completes a fresh listening, speaking, reading or writing task for «I will communicate without demanding native accent». Gesture, picture or word bank may support access, but the final choice or message must be their own. |
| Cierre | 10 min | With one new prompt, demonstrate «I will communicate without demanding native accent» by acting, selecting, saying or writing. Point to the heard, read or visual clue and repair one part after feedback. |

**Materiales y preparación:** mirror, sound cards, short chant and respectful pronunciation recording
