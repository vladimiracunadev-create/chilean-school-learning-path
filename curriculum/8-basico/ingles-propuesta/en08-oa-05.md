### Clase 1 de 5: Recognize purpose and context: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts {#cl-09035}
**Estado editorial:** Desarrollada con contenido específico.

**Propósito docente:** Help students recognize purpose and context: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts through meaningful English and evidence from the message.

**Meta para estudiantes:** Today I will recognize purpose and context: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts; I will show the clue or language choice that helped me communicate.

| Momento | Tiempo | Acción docente y experiencia |
|---|---:|---|
| Inicio | 10 min | Present short authorized literary and functional texts with numbered paragraphs, visuals and an essential glossary once. Students first respond with a choice, gesture, note or short chunk and then identify the clue they used. |
| Modelado | 20 min | Think aloud in accessible English to show how to recognize purpose and context: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts. Combine an exact clue with context and check meaning instead of translating every word. |
| Práctica guiada | 25 min | Pairs work with a second version of short authorized literary and functional texts with numbered paragraphs, visuals and an essential glossary. They exchange missing information, ask for clarification and improve one part after focused feedback. |
| Desempeño individual | 25 min | Each student completes a new task to recognize purpose and context: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts. A word bank or planning card is available, but the student selects language, communicates meaning and checks the result. |
| Cierre | 10 min | Give one short response for today's purpose and underline, point to or name the clue or language choice that supports it. |

**Materiales y preparación:** short authorized literary and functional texts with numbered paragraphs, visuals and an essential glossary; visible word bank, picture cards and optional audio replay; no account or personal information required.

### Insumo concreto y consigna

**Recurso listo para usar:** Two original 130-word texts on one fictional project—one literary and one functional—with headings, image captions, paragraph numbers and a short essential glossary.

**Consigna exacta:** Read for “recognize purpose and context: reading language functions in meaningful contexts”: state the purpose or main idea, cite two textual or visual clues and revise one answer after rereading a specific paragraph.

**Referencia para modelar y corregir:** The response distinguishes purpose, gist and detail, cites precise clues and coordinates words with layout or image rather than copying an unrelated sentence.

**Lista de preparación docente:**
- [ ] Preparar el insumo concreto sin datos personales ni material sin autorización.
- [ ] Ensayar la respuesta de referencia y anticipar al menos un error plausible.
- [ ] Definir cómo recogerá evidencia individual durante el desempeño.
- [ ] Comprobar accesibilidad, seguridad y alternativa sin conectividad.

**Pauta de evaluación de cuatro niveles:**

| Nivel | Descriptor observable |
|---|---|
| Aún no observable | No produce evidencia suficiente o responde sin relacionarla con la consigna. |
| En desarrollo | Responde parcialmente y necesita apoyo para usar el criterio o la evidencia. |
| Logrado | Responde la consigna, usa el criterio central y deja una evidencia comprensible. Referencia de esta clase: The response distinguishes purpose, gist and detail, cites precise clues and coordinates words with layout or image rather than copying an unrelated sentence. |
| Profundizado | Justifica, contrasta o transfiere la decisión a una condición nueva. |

| Distribución de 45 minutos | Acción imprescindible |
|---:|---|
| 5 min | Activación breve y lectura de la consigna exacta. |
| 8 min | Modelado con el insumo concreto y una decisión visible. |
| 12 min | Primer intento guiado y retroalimentación según un criterio. |
| 15 min | Desempeño individual con evidencia atribuible. |
| 5 min | Ticket, clasificación del nivel y decisión posterior. |


**Apoyo en el mismo OA:** Keep images and chunks visible, allow choral and paired rehearsal, reduce length and provide wait time; do not penalize accent, initial language mixing or self-correction.

**Profundización:** Change speaker, audience, quantity, place or action and adapt the message to the new communicative purpose.

**Evidencia:** Comprehensible oral, written, visual or embodied response for «recognize purpose and context: reading language functions in meaningful contexts» linked to a word, chunk or clue in the input.

**Criterios de éxito:** resuelve la consigna exacta de «recognize purpose and context: reading language functions in meaningful contexts»; usa al menos dos datos, rasgos o evidencias del insumo concreto; explica una decisión y revisa su respuesta ante una condición nueva.

**Decisión posterior:** Avanza cuando decisión, evidencia y explicación coinciden; si no, cambia el acceso o modela otro caso y vuelve a recoger evidencia sin etiquetar al estudiante.

**Adaptación a 45 minutos:** Conserva situación específica, modelado, práctica guiada, evidencia individual y cierre; reduce cantidad o turnos, no el criterio disciplinar.

**Tarea breve y flexible:** Observa, practica o registra una versión breve y segura con recursos disponibles. No requiere compras, internet, datos personales ni exposición familiar, corporal o cultural.

**Actividades complementarias (opcionales):**
- Recuperación: aísla una decisión o pista y vuelve luego a la tarea completa.
- Análisis de error: revisa un caso ficticio que muestra esta confusión: treat accent, speed or immediate accuracy as intelligence.
- Transferencia: cambia contexto, material, interlocutor, espacio o fuente y explica qué debe adaptarse.

**Control de dificultades en el aula**

| Dificultad observable | Acción inmediata | Cómo comprobar si funcionó |
|---|---|---|
| Repite o imita sin tomar una decisión | Ofrece dos alternativas y pide elegir una según el propósito específico. | Puede mostrar qué eligió, para qué y con qué evidencia. |
| Treat accent, speed or immediate accuracy as intelligence | Keep images and chunks visible, allow choral and paired rehearsal, reduce length and provide wait time; do not penalize accent, initial language mixing or self-correction. | Resuelve un caso nuevo sin repetir la confusión. |
| Se inhibe, queda expuesto o no accede al formato | Reduce exposición y ofrece una vía equivalente de participación, manteniendo el objetivo. | Produce evidencia propia mediante una vía accesible y segura. |

**Coordinación de roles profesionales:** El docente modela inglés comprensible; educación diferencial facilita acceso auditivo, visual, motor o de respuesta sin sustituir la intención comunicativa.

### Clase 5 de 5: Respond independently: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts {#cl-09039}
**Estado editorial:** Desarrollada con contenido específico.

**Propósito docente:** Help students respond independently: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts through meaningful English and evidence from the message.

**Meta para estudiantes:** Today I will respond independently: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts; I will show the clue or language choice that helped me communicate.

| Momento | Tiempo | Acción docente y experiencia |
|---|---:|---|
| Inicio | 10 min | Change the speaker, audience or purpose in short authorized literary and functional texts with numbered paragraphs, visuals and an essential glossary. Students predict which language and delivery choices should change. |
| Modelado | 20 min | Use short authorized literary and functional texts with numbered paragraphs, visuals and an essential glossary to plan, communicate and check. Keep meaning central; accent imitation, speed and instant perfection are not the goal. |
| Práctica guiada | 25 min | Pairs work with a second version of short authorized literary and functional texts with numbered paragraphs, visuals and an essential glossary. They exchange missing information, ask for clarification and improve one part after focused feedback. |
| Desempeño individual | 25 min | Each student completes a new task to respond independently: Reading language functions in meaningful contexts. A word bank or planning card is available, but the student selects language, communicates meaning and checks the result. |
| Cierre | 10 min | Give one short response for today's purpose and underline, point to or name the clue or language choice that supports it. |

**Materiales y preparación:** short authorized literary and functional texts with numbered paragraphs, visuals and an essential glossary; visible word bank, picture cards and optional audio replay; no account or personal information required.

### Insumo concreto y consigna

**Recurso listo para usar:** Two original 130-word texts on one fictional project—one literary and one functional—with headings, image captions, paragraph numbers and a short essential glossary.

**Consigna exacta:** Read for “respond independently: reading language functions in meaningful contexts”: state the purpose or main idea, cite two textual or visual clues and revise one answer after rereading a specific paragraph.

**Referencia para modelar y corregir:** The response distinguishes purpose, gist and detail, cites precise clues and coordinates words with layout or image rather than copying an unrelated sentence.

**Lista de preparación docente:**
- [ ] Preparar el insumo concreto sin datos personales ni material sin autorización.
- [ ] Ensayar la respuesta de referencia y anticipar al menos un error plausible.
- [ ] Definir cómo recogerá evidencia individual durante el desempeño.
- [ ] Comprobar accesibilidad, seguridad y alternativa sin conectividad.

**Profundización:** Change speaker, audience, quantity, place or action and adapt the message to the new communicative purpose.

**Evidencia:** Comprehensible oral, written, visual or embodied response for «respond independently: reading language functions in meaningful contexts» linked to a word, chunk or clue in the input.

**Criterios de éxito:** resuelve la consigna exacta de «respond independently: reading language functions in meaningful contexts»; usa al menos dos datos, rasgos o evidencias del insumo concreto; explica una decisión y revisa su respuesta ante una condición nueva.
