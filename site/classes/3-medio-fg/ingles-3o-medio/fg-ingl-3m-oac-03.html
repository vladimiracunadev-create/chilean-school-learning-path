3° medio · Formación General · Inglés 3º medio

Language resources for critical comprehension and production

FG-INGL-3M-OAC-03 · Objetivos de aprendizaje

Estado editorialDesarrolladaContenido disciplinar específico; pendiente de revisión humana.

Objetivo oficial

Qué se espera aprender

OA significa Objetivo de Aprendizaje: el resultado que debe alcanzar el estudiante. No es una actividad ni una clase; por eso este OA se desarrolla en una secuencia.

Utilizar su conocimiento del inglés en la comprensión y producción de textos orales y escritos breves y claros, con el fin de construir una postura personal crítica en contextos relacionados con sus intereses e inquietudes.

Derechos y procedencia: el código y la redacción del OA proceden de Currículum Nacional (MINEDUC), se muestran con enlace y fecha de consulta y no se declaran obra del proyecto ni se relicencian bajo CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

01

Clase 1 de 4

Activate purpose and prior knowledge: Language resources for critical comprehension and production

Desarrollada

Propósito docente: Help students activate purpose and prior knowledge: Language resources for critical comprehension and production through meaningful English, evidence from the input and purposeful revision.

Meta para estudiantes: Today I will activate purpose and prior knowledge: Language resources for critical comprehension and production; I will use evidence, communicate for a purpose and revise meaning when needed.

Inicio · 10 min

Present a short, original and attributed oral, written or multimodal text about language resources for critical comprehension and production once. Students identify purpose, central meaning and one clue before translating or preparing a full response.

Modelado · 20 min

Think aloud in accessible English to activate purpose and prior knowledge: Language resources for critical comprehension and production. Use an exact clue, useful language and context; address the misconception: memorize without adapting to purpose.

Práctica guiada · 25 min

Pairs complete an information-gap or comparison about language resources for critical comprehension and production, ask for clarification and revise one part for meaning and audience.

Desempeño individual · 25 min

Each student completes a new task to activate purpose and prior knowledge: Language resources for critical comprehension and production; they select support, communicate a position and repair meaning after checking the input.

Materiales y preparación

a short, original and attributed oral, written or multimodal text about language resources for critical comprehension and production; visible language support, planning grid and an offline alternative. Check attribution, accessibility and privacy.

Insumo concreto y consigna

Recurso listo para usar

Three original writing briefs for a real-world purpose, each with audience, model chunks, planning grid, source notes and a revision checklist that separates meaning from accuracy.

Consigna exacta

Work on “activate purpose and prior knowledge: language resources for critical comprehension and production”: identify purpose and audience, use two precise language or visual clues, communicate a complete response and revise one decision after feedback or rereading.

Referencia para modelar y corregir

A successful response communicates meaning for the stated audience, supports ideas with accurate clues, uses learned language flexibly and improves content or organization before surface editing; accent imitation is not required.

Preparación docente

  • Preparar el insumo concreto sin datos personales ni material sin autorización.
  • Ensayar la respuesta de referencia y anticipar al menos un error plausible.
  • Definir cómo recogerá evidencia individual durante el desempeño.
  • Comprobar accesibilidad, seguridad y alternativa sin conectividad.

Apoyo: Keep chunks and visual clues available, allow rehearsal and alternative response modes, and do not penalize accent, wait time or self-correction.

Profundización: Change speaker, worldview, audience or purpose and adapt language while preserving the central message.

Ticket de salida · 10 min

Give a short response for today's purpose and identify the clue or language choice that supports it.

Evidencia: Comprehensible individual response for «activate purpose and prior knowledge: language resources for critical comprehension and production» with input evidence, purposeful language and revision.

Criterios observables

  • completes «activate purpose and prior knowledge: language resources for critical comprehension and production» for the stated purpose
  • uses precise evidence or language from the input
  • communicates comprehensibly and revises one decision

Pauta de evaluación de cuatro niveles

NivelDescriptor observable
Aún no observableNo produce evidencia suficiente o responde sin relacionarla con la consigna.
En desarrolloResponde parcialmente y necesita apoyo para usar el criterio o la evidencia.
LogradoResponde la consigna, usa el criterio central y deja una evidencia comprensible. Referencia de esta clase: A successful response communicates meaning for the stated audience, supports ideas with accurate clues, uses learned language flexibly and improves content or organization before surface editing; accent imitation is not required.
ProfundizadoJustifica, contrasta o transfiere la decisión a una condición nueva.

Distribución de 45 minutos

TiempoAcción preservada
5 minActivación breve y lectura de la consigna exacta.
8 minModelado con el insumo concreto y una decisión visible.
12 minPrimer intento guiado y retroalimentación según un criterio.
15 minDesempeño individual con evidencia atribuible.
5 minTicket, clasificación del nivel y decisión posterior.

Decisión posterior: Move on when purpose, evidence and message align; otherwise change access, model another example and collect new evidence without labelling the learner.

Si dispone de 45 minutos: Keep the specific input, brief modelling, guided rehearsal, individual evidence and exit response; reduce length or turns, not the communicative demand.

Consolidación

Tarea breve y flexible

Observe or create a short, safe example using available resources. No purchase, account, internet access or disclosure of personal information is required.

Banco opcional

Actividades complementarias

  • Recovery: reduce input length or available choices, then return to the complete communicative task.
  • Error analysis: revise a fictional response that shows this misconception: memorize without adapting to purpose.
  • Transfer: change audience, viewpoint, mode or purpose and adapt the message.
