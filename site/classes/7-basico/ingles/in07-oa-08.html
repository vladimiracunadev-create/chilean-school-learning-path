7° básico · Inglés

Language functions in speaking

IN07 OA 08 · Comunicación oral

Estado editorialDesarrolladaContenido disciplinar específico; pendiente de revisión humana.

Objetivo oficial

Qué se espera aprender

OA significa Objetivo de Aprendizaje: el resultado que debe alcanzar el estudiante. No es una actividad ni una clase; por eso este OA se desarrolla en una secuencia.

Demostrar conocimiento y uso del lenguaje en conversaciones, discusiones y exposiciones por medio de las siguientes funciones: Expresar cantidades, contar y enumerar; por ejemplo: there is /are many/ much/eighty/a hundred/some people/water; too. Identificar y describir objetos, deportes y hobbies; por ejemplo: these/those cars are fast; it's a plastic bottle. Expresarse sobre actividades; por ejemplo: riding/skating is cool/boring. Describir estado de ánimo; por ejemplo: he looks tired/hungry/nervous/good; I'm angry about/with... Expresar sugerencias, obligación y prohibición; por ejemplo: I must practice; you mustn't ride without a helmet; we could..; Don't ... Expresar intenciones y necesidades; por ejemplo: we need/want/forget to recycle . Demostrar posesión; por ejemplo: they are the students' instruments . Expresarse con claridad, usando palabras y expresiones de uso común, sinónimos, palabras compuestas; por ejemplo: afraid of flying, give advice on...; laugh at..;ask for....; take a break; have fun/a good time; I want/don't want...; see you later/soon; make a mistake/ plans/friends; for example; outdoor. Señalar tiempo, el grado y el modo en que ocurren las acciones; por ejemplo: he won the race yesterday; she sings quietly; they run very fast; every week; a bit/little, very...; suddenly. Formular preguntas y justificar una opinión; por ejemplo: why do you like football? I like football because it's a team sport. Explicar causa y efecto; por ejemplo: ice melts if you heat it. Formular y responder preguntas sobre rutinas y acciones presentes y pasadas; por ejemplo: does/did he cook? Yes/no, he does/doesn't/did/didn't. Unir ideas; por ejemplo: I like that film too; do you want the book or the magazine?; First, second, next, finally; he felt nervous before/after the test. Describir acciones que interrumpen u ocurren simultáneamente en el pasado; por ejemplo: I was playing football in the yard, when it started to rain; while he was cycling, she was listening to music.

Derechos y procedencia: el código y la redacción del OA proceden de Currículum Nacional (MINEDUC), se muestran con enlace y fecha de consulta y no se declaran obra del proyecto ni se relicencian bajo CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

01

Clase 1 de 5

Exchange information and quantities

Desarrollada

Propósito docente: Help students exchange information and quantities through meaningful, accessible English and evidence from the message.

Meta para estudiantes: Today I will exchange information and quantities; I will show the clue or language choice that helped me communicate.

Inicio · 10 min

Present picture scenes, schedules, quantities and fictional event cards with a functional language bank rather than a full script once without translating it in full. Students first respond with a choice, gesture, note or short chunk and then point to the clue they used.

Modelado · 20 min

Think aloud in clear, accessible English and show how to exchange information and quantities. Point to the exact clue, combine it with context and check the meaning instead of translating every word.

Práctica guiada · 25 min

Pairs work with a second version of picture scenes, schedules, quantities and fictional event cards with a functional language bank rather than a full script. Each student contributes information, asks for clarification and improves one part of the message after focused feedback.

Desempeño individual · 25 min

Each student completes a new task to exchange information and quantities. They may use a word bank or planning card, but must select language, communicate meaning and check the result independently.

Materiales y preparación

picture scenes, schedules, quantities and fictional event cards with a functional language bank rather than a full script; visible word bank, picture cards and optional audio replay; no account or personal information required.

Insumo concreto y consigna

Recurso listo para usar

Information-gap cards for two fictional school projects, clarification stems, key-word planning card and a three-panel paper slide deck.

Consigna exacta

Work on “exchange information and quantities”: exchange missing information, ask one clarification question and adapt a short message for a partner or small audience.

Referencia para modelar y corregir

The message is understandable, responds to the other speaker, uses support without reading a full script and repairs meaning when needed; accent imitation is not required.

Preparación docente

  • Preparar el insumo concreto sin datos personales ni material sin autorización.
  • Ensayar la respuesta de referencia y anticipar al menos un error plausible.
  • Definir cómo recogerá evidencia individual durante el desempeño.
  • Comprobar accesibilidad, seguridad y alternativa sin conectividad.

Apoyo: Keep images and chunks visible, allow choral and paired rehearsal, reduce length and provide wait time; do not penalize accent, initial language mixing or self-correction.

Profundización: Change speaker, audience, quantity, place or action and adapt the message to the new communicative purpose.

Ticket de salida · 10 min

Give one short response for today's purpose and underline, point to or name the clue or language choice that supports it.

Evidencia: Comprehensible oral, written, visual or embodied response for «exchange information and quantities» linked to a word, chunk or clue in the input.

Criterios observables

  • resuelve la consigna exacta de «exchange information and quantities»
  • usa al menos dos datos, rasgos o evidencias del insumo concreto
  • explica una decisión y revisa su respuesta ante una condición nueva

Pauta de evaluación de cuatro niveles

NivelDescriptor observable
Aún no observableNo produce evidencia suficiente o responde sin relacionarla con la consigna.
En desarrolloResponde parcialmente y necesita apoyo para usar el criterio o la evidencia.
LogradoResponde la consigna, usa el criterio central y deja una evidencia comprensible. Referencia de esta clase: The message is understandable, responds to the other speaker, uses support without reading a full script and repairs meaning when needed; accent imitation is not required.
ProfundizadoJustifica, contrasta o transfiere la decisión a una condición nueva.

Si dispone de 45 minutos: Conserva situación específica, modelado, práctica guiada, evidencia individual y cierre; reduce cantidad o turnos, no el criterio disciplinar.

Clase 2 de 5

Clase 3 de 5

Express obligation, possibility and preference

Desarrollada

Meta para estudiantes: Today I will express obligation, possibility and preference; I will show the clue or language choice that helped me communicate.

Clase 4 de 5

Desarrollada

Clase 5 de 5

Combine functions in a real interaction

Desarrollada

Propósito docente: Help students combine functions in a real interaction through meaningful, accessible English and evidence from the message.

Meta para estudiantes: Today I will combine functions in a real interaction; I will show the clue or language choice that helped me communicate.

Inicio · 10 min

Change the speaker, audience or purpose in picture scenes, schedules, quantities and fictional event cards with a functional language bank rather than a full script. Students predict which language and delivery choices should change.

Modelado · 20 min

Use picture scenes, schedules, quantities and fictional event cards with a functional language bank rather than a full script to model planning, communicating and checking. Highlight meaning, not speed, accent imitation or immediate accuracy.

Práctica guiada · 25 min

Pairs work with a second version of picture scenes, schedules, quantities and fictional event cards with a functional language bank rather than a full script. Each student contributes information, asks for clarification and improves one part of the message after focused feedback.

Desempeño individual · 25 min

Each student completes a new task to combine functions in a real interaction. They may use a word bank or planning card, but must select language, communicate meaning and check the result independently.

Materiales y preparación

picture scenes, schedules, quantities and fictional event cards with a functional language bank rather than a full script; visible word bank, picture cards and optional audio replay; no account or personal information required.

Insumo concreto y consigna

Recurso listo para usar

Information-gap cards for two fictional school projects, clarification stems, key-word planning card and a three-panel paper slide deck.

Consigna exacta

Work on “combine functions in a real interaction”: exchange missing information, ask one clarification question and adapt a short message for a partner or small audience.

Referencia para modelar y corregir

The message is understandable, responds to the other speaker, uses support without reading a full script and repairs meaning when needed; accent imitation is not required.

Preparación docente

  • Preparar el insumo concreto sin datos personales ni material sin autorización.
  • Ensayar la respuesta de referencia y anticipar al menos un error plausible.
  • Definir cómo recogerá evidencia individual durante el desempeño.
  • Comprobar accesibilidad, seguridad y alternativa sin conectividad.

Apoyo: Keep images and chunks visible, allow choral and paired rehearsal, reduce length and provide wait time; do not penalize accent, initial language mixing or self-correction.

Profundización: Change speaker, audience, quantity, place or action and adapt the message to the new communicative purpose.

Ticket de salida · 10 min

Give one short response for today's purpose and underline, point to or name the clue or language choice that supports it.

Evidencia: Comprehensible oral, written, visual or embodied response for «combine functions in a real interaction» linked to a word, chunk or clue in the input.

Criterios observables

  • resuelve la consigna exacta de «combine functions in a real interaction»
  • usa al menos dos datos, rasgos o evidencias del insumo concreto
  • explica una decisión y revisa su respuesta ante una condición nueva

Decisión posterior: Avanza cuando decisión, evidencia y explicación coinciden; si no, cambia el acceso o modela otro caso y vuelve a recoger evidencia sin etiquetar al estudiante.

Si dispone de 45 minutos: Conserva situación específica, modelado, práctica guiada, evidencia individual y cierre; reduce cantidad o turnos, no el criterio disciplinar.

Consolidación

Tarea breve y flexible

Observa, practica o registra una versión breve y segura con recursos disponibles. No requiere compras, internet, datos personales ni exposición familiar, corporal o cultural.

Banco opcional

Actividades complementarias

  • Recuperación: aísla una decisión o pista y vuelve luego a la tarea completa.
  • Análisis de error: revisa un caso ficticio que muestra esta confusión: complete grammar drills without a communicative purpose or demand perfect forms before allowing interaction.
  • Transferencia: cambia contexto, material, interlocutor, espacio o fuente y explica qué debe adaptarse.
